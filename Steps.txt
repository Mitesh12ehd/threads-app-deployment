Any Internal Service

First list services:

kubectl get svc -A

Then forward:

kubectl port-forward -n <namespace> svc/<service-name> <local-port>:<service-port>

Example:

kubectl port-forward -n default svc/thread-app-service 8080:80

Access:

http://localhost:8080
